Most ecommerce brands don't have a traffic problem.

Founder-style LinkedIn post · thought leadership

Most ecommerce brands don't have a traffic problem.

They have a “give me more traffic” problem.

When sales slow down, the reflex is predictable: increase spend, test another audience, change the campaign, blame the algorithm.

Sometimes the account is fine.

The offer isn't.

Google can put your product in front of someone who is already looking for it. It can't make a weak reason to buy suddenly feel compelling.

So before I touch bids or budgets, I want to know:

Would I stop for this ad?
Would I understand the offer in five seconds?
Would I know why this product instead of the ten sitting next to it?

If the answer is no, another 20% in ad spend isn't a growth strategy.

It's a more expensive way to find the same problem.

У більшості e-commerce брендів проблема не в трафіку.

Проблема — у пропозиції.

Google може показати ваш продукт людині, яка вже його шукає. Але він не може зробити слабку причину для покупки переконливою.

Якщо відповідь «ні», ще 20% рекламного бюджету — це не стратегія зростання.

Це просто дорожчий спосіб знайти ту саму проблему.

If your agency starts with CTR, ask one question.

Short-form script · direct-response thought leadership

Hook
If your Google Ads agency starts the meeting with CTR, ask them one question: “So what?”
Beat 1
CTR is useful. I look at it too. But a click isn't the goal. Revenue is. Profit is. Growth you can actually keep is.
Beat 2
You can have a beautiful dashboard full of green numbers while the business underneath it is getting worse.
Beat 3
So when someone tells you, “CTR is up 18%,” don't celebrate yet.
Payoff
Ask: “Did the extra clicks make us more money?” That's the number I'd rather build a strategy around.
Close
Optimize the account, sure. Just don't forget you're running a business, not a spreadsheet.

Three workout descriptions — Gym, Pilates, Yoga

Warm, human, empowering. No fluff. No fitness jargon.

Gym Strength & Power

"You're not here to look like someone else. You're here to be stronger than you were last week."

This isn't about ego. It's about showing up, picking up something heavy, and putting it down with control. Each set builds more than muscle — it builds discipline. Track your reps. Add a little more weight. Rest 60 seconds. Repeat. By week four, you'll notice the difference in your posture, your energy, and the way you carry yourself.

⏱ 45–60 min 🏋️ Dumbbells or barbell 📊 All levels
Pilates Core & Control

"Move with intention, not momentum."

Pilates isn't about how fast you move — it's about how precisely you control each motion. Every inhale prepares you; every exhale engages your deep core. This is where posture improves, back pain fades, and you finally understand what "alignment" actually means. No rushing. No ego. Just you, your mat, and a deeper connection to your body.

⏱ 30–40 min 🧘 Mat 📊 All levels
Yoga Flow & Let Go

"Your practice, your pace."

Step onto the mat, close your eyes, and breathe. This isn't about touching your toes — it's about the journey there. Each pose asks you to be present, patient, and honest with yourself. Some days you'll flow effortlessly; other days you'll rest in child's pose. Both are progress. Both are enough.

⏱ 30–50 min 🧘 Mat 📊 All levels

Practice — EssayПрактика — есе Own writing, 2026Власний текст, 2026

Notes from someone who writes in two languages: code, and everything else

My code reads like it has a personality, and my interface copy has more rhythm than it strictly needs. I think that's because I don't see a line between them — writing a function and writing a sentence are the same problem, stated differently: say exactly what's true, cut what isn't, and leave room for the next person to understand it without you in the room. Seven languages, seven programming languages — each one changes the shape of what you can say.

Нотатки людини, яка пише двома мовами: код і все інше

Мій код читається так, наче має характер, а мій інтерфейсний текст має більше ритму, ніж потрібно. Думаю, це тому, що я не бачу межі між ними — написати функцію і написати речення це та сама задача, сформульована по-різному: сказати точно те, що правда, прибрати зайве і залишити місце, щоб наступна людина зрозуміла це без мене поруч. Сім мов, сім мов програмування — кожна змінює форму того, що можна сказати.

Language exchange & international communication

Real practice: daily conversations with native speakers through apps and social media.

Since 2024, I've been actively practicing foreign languages through real conversations with native speakers from around the world. I use apps like Tandem, HelloTalk, and social media platforms to connect with people, exchange cultures, and improve my speaking and writing skills.

These aren't just vocabulary drills — they're real, unscripted conversations. I've discussed everything from daily life to mental health, technology, and AI. This experience taught me to adapt my communication style to different cultural contexts and language levels.

"Every conversation is a chance to understand someone else's world — and to express my own more clearly."
