# Instagram Captions — Independent Bookstore

**Project:** Practice (Imaginary Client)
**Goal:** Write warm, engaging captions for a small independent bookstore
**Tone:** Personal, inviting, community-focused

---

## Captions

### 1. Restock Announcement
> "We restock at 10am. By 11, half this table is gone. Come see what's left."

### 2. Staff Picks
> "A staff pick is just a recommendation from someone who read it at 1am and couldn't put it down. This week: three of those."

### 3. Slow Reading
> "Not every book needs a plot twist. Some just need you to sit with a good sentence for a while. Aisle 3 has a few."

### 4. Independent Bookstore Day
> "We're small. We're local. And we're here because you keep showing up. Thank you for making this more than a store — it's a community."

### 5. New Arrivals
> "Fresh off the truck: 25 new titles. Some from authors you know, some you've never heard of. We recommend the ones you've never heard of — they're the ones that stay with you."

### 6. Rainy Day
> "Rainy days were made for reading. Grab a book, find a corner, and let the world outside fade. Open until 8pm. Coffee is on the house for anyone who walks in with wet shoes."

### 7. Book Club Announcement
> "Next month: books that feel like a warm hug on a cold day. We'll have tea, cookies, and a lot of opinions. Everyone welcome. Thursday, 7pm."

### 8. Closing Time
> "Another day. Another stack of books finding their way home. See you tomorrow at 9am — we'll have the coffee waiting."

---

## Why These Work

- **Human voice** — Feels like a person, not a corporation
- **Story-driven** — Each caption tells a small story
- **Call to action** — Invites engagement without being pushy
- **Community focus** — Makes readers feel like they belong

---

*Written to make people feel something — and maybe buy a book.*
