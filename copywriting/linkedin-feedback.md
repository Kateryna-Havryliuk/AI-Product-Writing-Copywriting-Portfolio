# LinkedIn Post: Why I Don't Take Feedback Personally

---

"This text doesn't work. Rewrite it."

When I first heard that, it felt like the end. Failure and shame. I thought: "I tried so hard! What's wrong with me?"

But then I realized: edits aren't about me. They're about the text.

When I worked at the library, I had to edit visitor documents. I didn't tell them to rewrite everything when I saw tautology or small mistakes — I improved the text while keeping its essence. It never crossed my mind that they were illiterate or incompetent. That's how we should treat our own texts too.

Edits are a tool, not a verdict. They're steps toward success and professionalism — steps you need to climb to see your peak.

How do you react to feedback on your text?
