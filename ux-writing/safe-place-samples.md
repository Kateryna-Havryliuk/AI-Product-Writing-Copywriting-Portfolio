# Safe Place — UX Writing Samples

**Project:** Safe Place — Mental Health Web App
**Role:** UX Writer & Creator
**Context:** A web application that uses generative AI to provide psycho-emotional support to users and analyze their emotional state.

---

## Onboarding

**Welcome screen:**
> "Welcome to Safe Place. No judgment. No pressure. Just a space to check in with yourself."

**First-time user prompt:**
> "We all have days that feel heavier than others. Start by typing how you're feeling right now — in a word, a sentence, or just a sigh."

---

## Daily Check-in

**Main prompt:**
> "How are you feeling today? Take a moment. Then write what comes to mind."

**Gentle nudge (if user hesitates):**
> "Not sure where to start? That's okay. You can write about anything — or nothing at all. The space is yours."

---

## Emotional Analysis & Response

**When the user expresses sadness:**
> "That sounds like a lot to carry. Want to explore this further together? Or just sit with it for a moment — I'm here either way."

**When the user expresses anxiety:**
> "I hear the worry in your words. Let's slow down for a moment. What's one small thing you can see around you right now?"

**When the user expresses joy:**
> "That warmth in your words made me smile. It's good to share moments like this — thank you for letting me in."

---

## Crisis Detection

**When crisis is detected:**
> "What you're sharing right now sounds really heavy — and I want to make sure you're safe. Please know that you don't have to go through this alone. Here are some contacts that can support you right now, 24/7:

> 📞 0 800 100 102 (Ukraine)
> 📞 116 123 (Emotional support line)
> 📞 Emergency services: 112

> I'm here to listen, but these professionals are trained to help in ways I can't. Please consider reaching out to them — they truly care.

---

## Empty State

**When the user has no conversation history:**
> "Nothing to see here yet. Every journey starts with a single step — or a single word. I'll be here when you're ready."

**When the user has no tasks:**
> "Your list is empty. That's a good thing — it means you're all caught up. Come back whenever you need."

---

## Error States

**Network error:**
> "Looks like we lost connection. Take a breath — I'll be back as soon as you're reconnected. If this persists, try refreshing the page."

**API error:**
> "Something went wrong on my side. Please don't worry — your words are safe. Try again in a moment, and I'll be here to respond."

---

## Settings

**Privacy notice:**
> "Your conversations are private and secure. We store only what you choose to share — no one else will read your words unless you decide otherwise."

**Account deletion:**
> "We're sorry to see you go. If you ever need support, remember you can always come back. This space will be here for you."

---

## Key Principles I Followed

1. **Empathy over clinical tone** — Users come for support, not a diagnosis
2. **Clarity over complexity** — Simple language that anyone can understand
3. **Safety first** — Crisis detection with clear, actionable steps
4. **Human warmth** — The app should feel like a compassionate listener, not a robot

---

All copy was written for human connection — making complex technology feel warm and approachable.
