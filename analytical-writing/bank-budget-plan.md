# 2026 Bank Budget Plan — Executive Summary

**Project:** 2026 Bank Budget Plan
**Role:** Analytical Writer
**Audience:** Industry experts and bank management
**Goal:** Turn complex financial data into a clear, readable document

---

## Overview

This project involved creating a full 2026 budget plan for a banking institution, covering income, expenses, inventory, and supply chain. Mathematical optimization methods were applied to ensure financial efficiency.

**Technical half:** Building a financial model using Excel and SQL to analyze data and optimize resource allocation.

**Writing half:** Translating complex financial data into a clear, structured narrative for non-specialists.

**Result:** The final document was presented to invited industry experts and approved with distinction.

---

## Key Findings

### Income Analysis
- Projected income growth of 12% compared to 2025
- Main drivers: increased investment portfolio and higher net interest margin

### Expense Optimization
- Identified 8% potential cost reduction through supply chain optimization
- Recommended restructuring of operational expenses in non-core areas

### Risk Assessment
- Credit risk remains stable at 2.3%
- Liquidity ratio maintained at 18.5% (above regulatory minimum)
- Capital adequacy ratio: 14.2% (exceeds Basel III requirements)

---

## Methodological Approach

### Optimization Methods Used
- Linear programming for resource allocation
- Scenario analysis for stress testing
- Monte Carlo simulation for risk assessment

### Data Sources
- Historical financial data (2022–2025)
- Market forecasts from industry analysts
- Regulatory requirements from the National Bank of Ukraine

---

## Key Recommendations

1. **Invest in digital infrastructure** — Reduce operational costs by 5% through automation
2. **Expand into SME lending** — Higher margins and lower competition
3. **Optimize supply chain** — Consolidate suppliers to reduce procurement costs
4. **Increase capital reserves** — Prepare for potential economic volatility in 2026
