# News Rewrite: Meta Pays $16B

**Original source:** Bloomberg

---

**My rewrite:**

Meta agreed to pay over $16 billion to settle lawsuits with 29 US states over teen safety on social media. The company will add restrictions for young users but denies all accusations.

Representatives of 29 states joined the federal court case in Oakland, California. They accuse Meta of violating consumer protection and privacy laws. The company must now limit time minors spend watching content, prevent them from disabling safety settings without parental consent, and strengthen age verification tools.

An independent auditor will monitor compliance and may provide recommendations to the states.

Meta and TikTok tried to appeal the lawsuits, citing a law that protects online services from liability for user content. But the court ruled this protection does not cancel the legal process itself.
