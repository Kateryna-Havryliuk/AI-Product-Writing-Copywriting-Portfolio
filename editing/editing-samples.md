# Editing: Corporate Jargon → Clear Copy

## Example 1

**Before:**
"We are a leading company in the field of providing services for website and app development. We offer our clients high-quality services at affordable prices."

**After:**
"We develop websites and apps for businesses of any scale — from a coffee shop to an international company. 25+ projects since 2019. Budget from $500 to $1000."

---

## Example 2

**Before:**
"Our specialists have many years of experience in the IT sphere."

**After:**
"Working since 2019. 25+ launched projects."

---

## Example 3

**Before:**
"You will be provided with a full range of services for the development, implementation, and support of software products."

**After:**
"We develop, launch, and support your software."

---

**Changes:**
- "high-quality services" → "25+ projects" (numbers instead of claims)
- "many years of experience" → "since 2019" (specifics)
- "full range of services" → "develop, launch, support" (verbs are stronger)
- 65 words → 38 words (40% shorter)
